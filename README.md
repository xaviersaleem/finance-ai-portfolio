# Finance AI & Analytics Portfolio

Selected finance automation and business intelligence work, ranked by finance role relevance, practical business value, and demand for planning, data, systems, and AI-enabled workflow skills.

The archive contained many overlapping tutorial exercises. This repository includes two of the most complete, differentiated examples. Tutorial prompt PDFs and local dependency/cache files are omitted. All included companies and financial data are fictional examples for portfolio demonstration; validate independently before any real financial decisions.

## Portfolio ranking

1. **Finance Operations Decision Support Team** — New. Role-based finance analyst, FP&A strategy, and CFO advisory workflow with Python scripts, sample workbooks, and board recommendation. [Project files](finance-operations-decision-support/)

2. **FP&A Budget, Actual & Forecast Model** — Existing. Driver-based planning, scenarios, variance analysis, and management reporting. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xaviersaleem/fpna-budget-actual-forecast-model)

3. **CFO Dashboard in Power BI** — New. Interactive CFO reporting prototype covering financial performance, growth and retention, profitability and cost, and cash outlook. Includes PBIP, semantic model, and synthetic source workbook. [Project files](cfo-powerbi-dashboard/)

4. **Executive Finance Power BI Dashboard** — Existing. Interactive budget-versus-actual reporting and finance KPIs. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xaviersaleem/executive-finance-powerbi-dashboard)

5. **Finance ERP Management System** — Existing. Accounting processes, controls, and integrated reporting. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xaviersaleem/finance-erp-management-system)

6. **Three-Statement Financial Model** — Existing. Integrated income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, and scenarios. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xaviersaleem/three-statement-financial-model)

7. **Strategic Business Case & Investment Analysis** — Existing. NPV, IRR, sensitivity, and investment recommendation. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xaviersaleem/strategic-business-case-investment-analysis)

## Skills represented

FP&A, financial analysis, Python, pandas, Excel automation, Power BI, semantic modeling, TMDL, finance systems, investment analysis, executive reporting, and AI-assisted finance workflows.
