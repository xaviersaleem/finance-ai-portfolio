# Deck Structure — Board_Recommendation_Pack.pptx

Slide-by-slide layout for the board pack. The builder script assembles the slides and pre-fills evidence pulled from the workbooks; the CFO replaces every `[CFO: …]` prompt with director-ready prose. Save to `Final Deliverables/Board_Recommendation_Pack.pptx`.

## General standards
- 16:9, clean and uncluttered — this is a board document, not an analyst pack.
- One idea per slide; headline as an assertion (a takeaway), not a label.
- Quantified, concise bullets (aim ≤ 6 per slide, one line each). No jargon.
- The recommendation appears on Slide 1 and Slide 8 — consistent wording.
- Numbers must tie exactly to `Expansion_Business_Case.xlsx` and `Store_Analysis_Report.xlsx`.
- Neutral, professional palette; consistent fonts; no placeholder text in the final file.

## Slide order
0. Title slide (Board Recommendation — Crave Kitchen Group Expansion; CFO Advisor; date)
1. Executive Summary
2. Strategic Assessment
3. Financial Assessment
4. Major Risks
5. Major Opportunities
6. Recommendation
7. Supporting Rationale
8. Final Decision

---

### Title slide
Title, subtitle ("Should we open 10 new locations next year?"), prepared-by CFO Advisor, date, confidentiality note.

### 1. Executive Summary
The whole story on one slide.
- The question, and your one-line answer (the recommendation) up top.
- 3–5 bullets: state of the base (worker 1), the 10-store economics (NPV / ROI / payback), the headline risk, the strategic fit.
- A KPI strip for the 10-store scenario: capex, NPV, ROI, payback, pass/fail vs policy.

### 2. Strategic Assessment
- Does opening 10 advance the strategy (from the Executive Briefing)?
- Strengths it builds on (proven regions, unit economics, brand) vs stretch.
- `[CFO: verdict on strategic fit — strong / qualified / weak, and why.]`

### 3. Financial Assessment
- 10-store scenario table: capex, NPV @ hurdle, ROI, IRR, payback, profitability index, pass/fail.
- Brief scenario context (how 10 compares to 5 and 15, if available).
- Base health one-liner from worker 1 (blended margin, consistency).
- `[CFO: is the financial case attractive, marginal, or weak — and is the base strong enough to expand from?]`

### 4. Major Risks
- 3–5 top risks (execution of simultaneous openings, demand/ramp, cost inflation, concentration, financing), each with a one-line mitigation.
- Flag the variable the case is most sensitive to (from worker 2's sensitivity).

### 5. Major Opportunities
- 3–5 upsides (market capture, strong-region replication, margin headroom, momentum/brand).
- Tie to worker 1 opportunities and worker 2 forecasts.

### 6. Recommendation
- The recommendation stated plainly: **proceed / proceed with conditions / do not proceed this year**.
- If conditions: list them (phasing, financing gate, target regions, KPI gates).
- `[CFO: 2–3 sentences of reasoning — risk-adjusted return + strategic fit + capital discipline.]`

### 7. Supporting Rationale
- The evidence chain, explicitly citing prior workers: base health (worker 1) → expansion economics and scenario read (worker 2) → board framework criteria → your judgment.
- Why this recommendation over the alternatives (the other scenarios / delay).

### 8. Final Decision
Must answer the question outright.
- Large, unambiguous statement: **"Recommendation: [YES / YES, WITH CONDITIONS / NO] — Crave Kitchen Group [should / should not] open 10 new locations next year."**
- One line of the single most important reason.
- If conditional: the gating conditions in one line. If any next steps/approvals, name them.
